# FVL members participated in CSIG 2025, engaging in extensive discussions with numerous experts and scholars.
From September 19 to 21, 2025, the 21st Young Scientists Conference of CSIG was successfully held in Qingdao. The conference featured four special lectures, 30 thematic sub-forums, eight workshops and service forums (including academic and young scholar sessions), and eight young scholar forums, hosting over 200 high-level academic presentations in total. This vibrant academic gathering attracted more than 2,000 experts, scholars, and young researchers from academia and industry, along with representatives from over 350 institutions.

Two PhD students from our research group participated in the event. By attending the tutorials and workshops, they gained a clearer understanding of industry dynamics and academic frontiers. The diverse presentations allowed them to grasp the current state of research in various fields and inspired their own research.

FVL leader Prof. Zhi Jin chaired the National Natural Science Foundation Young Student Forum at the conference. The forum not only highlighted diverse perspectives from young scholars and students on cutting-edge research, but also provided a platform for in-depth communication and experience sharing.

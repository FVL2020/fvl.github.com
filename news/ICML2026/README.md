# Our Paper "Unifying Heterogeneous Degradations: Uncertainty-Aware Diffusion Bridge Model for All-in-One Image Restoration" Presented at ICML 2026.

A PhD student Luwei Tu from our lab, attended the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2026), held from July 6 to July 11, 2026, at the COEX Convention & Exhibition Center in Seoul, South Korea. As one of the world’s premier academic venues for artificial intelligence and machine learning, the conference brought together top researchers, scholars, and industry practitioners across the globe to showcase cutting-edge breakthroughs ranging from foundational machine learning theory to applied generative AI and computer vision.

The conference journey began on July 6 with on-site registration and tutorial sessions, offering an early opportunity to connect with international peers. The main program officially kicked off on July 7 with the opening ceremony, which shared key insights into the conference's submission statistics, review process, and overall paper acceptance landscape. The subsequent days featured keynote speeches and technical oral sessions spotlighting frontier directions in deep learning, alongside extensive poster sessions that fostered meaningful academic exchange across diverse research topics. During the interactive poster session, the lab's latest accepted work, titled "Unifying Heterogeneous Degradations: Uncertainty-Aware Diffusion Bridge Model for All-in-One Image Restoration," was presented to the community. The paper drew considerable interest from attendees working on image restoration, diffusion models, and inverse problems. In addition to inspiring fruitful technical discussions, the engagement helped identify several limitations and areas for improvement in the current version of the manuscript.

Attending ICML 2026 proved to be an enriching experience, providing great academic visibility for the lab's research as well as critical insights and fresh inspiration for future project iterations.
